Questions for The Gatekeeper

Who is The Gatekeeper?

He is the keeper of the rules, the numbers, the colours, and the black hole. That is all you need to know.

How do I receive my number?

The Gatekeeper assigns it. You will accept it promptly and refrain from asking whether another number was available.

May I choose my cute colour?

No. The colour is allocated by The Gatekeeper. It is cute by definition, and his definition is final.

What happens after three lettuces?

The Gatekeeper appears and commands you to come closer. This is not a suggestion.

What happens after nine lettuces?

The instruction changes. Never come closer. Keep hopping and do not demand consistency.

What happens if I fail?

You may be called a maggot. You may be banished to a black hole. The appeals process is also located in the black hole.

Can The Gatekeeper be reasoned with?

You are welcome to try. The Gatekeeper enjoys optimism almost as much as he enjoys banishment.
