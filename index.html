A Small Course With a Big Heart

The pop-up course is designed to feel calm, secure, and approachable for first-time hoppers.

Your bunny sets the pace. Sitting, sniffing, turning around, or declining to hop are all perfectly acceptable results.

  • Short and easy for beginners
  • Fully enclosed and escape-resistant
  • Set on a non-slip, rabbit-safe surface
  • Away from loud music, dogs, crowds, and sudden noises
  • Made with lightweight obstacles that cannot injure a rabbit
  • Cleaned between participants
  • Supervised by The Gatekeeper, with each owner present
  • Optional at every stage

A Little Romaine, If Their Person Approves

Fresh romaine can be used as a small reward, but only with the owner’s permission. Owners know their rabbits’ diets and health needs best.

  • Use washed, fresh romaine
  • Offer only a small amount
  • Do not use iceberg lettuce
  • Provide no treats without the owner’s approval
  • Make clean drinking water available
  • Allow owners to supply their rabbit’s preferred treat instead

When in doubt about food or physical activity, owners should consult their rabbit’s veterinarian.

Who Can Participate?

The activity is intended for healthy, socialized pet rabbits whose owners believe they will be comfortable in the environment.

  • Rabbit arrives in a secure carrier
  • The Gatekeeper supervises while the owner stays with their rabbit
  • Rabbit appears calm and comfortable
  • Owner approves all food rewards
  • Rabbit can leave immediately if they show stress
  • No participation by sick, injured, very young, elderly, pregnant, or visibly distressed rabbits
!

Know the stress signs

Freezing, repeated escape attempts, flattened posture, rapid breathing, aggression, or refusing food.

Participation should stop immediately if stress is observed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my rabbit need experience?

No. The course is short and beginner-friendly. Your rabbit is also welcome to simply look, sniff, or decide not to participate.

Will my rabbit be forced to jump?

Never. Rabbits are not pushed, pulled, chased, or forced. They choose whether to explore or hop, and can stop at any moment.

What should I bring?

Bring your rabbit in a secure carrier, plus any familiar comfort items, water setup, and an approved favourite treat you would like used.

Can children handle the rabbit?

The Gatekeeper supervises the course, while the rabbit’s adult owner decides who may handle them and stays present at all times. Calm, safe handling and the rabbit’s comfort come first.

What if my rabbit becomes nervous?

Stop immediately and return them to the security of their carrier or another calm space. Leaving the activity is always okay.

Is iceberg lettuce used?

No. Only a small amount of washed, fresh romaine may be offered, with the owner’s permission.

Is this a professional competition?

No. This is a friendly, informal community activity, not a sanctioned sporting event. The rabbit’s comfort matters more than completing the course.
